## Hardship support

Hardship support is offered to customers who face temporary financial challenges. The goal is to provide relief while preserving the credit relationship.

Customers can begin the process by speaking with a customer service representative. They will guide them through required steps and gather relevant information.

- Submit a hardship request form.  
- Provide proof of financial hardship, such as recent pay statements or a brief letter.  
- Discuss a temporary adjustment to the payment schedule.

A hardship plan may pause payments for up to 3 months. During this pause, the account remains in good standing, and no late fees accrue.

After the pause period, the customer may resume normal payments or negotiate a new payment schedule. If circumstances improve, the original terms may be reinstated.
