## Drug tiers

In our pharmacy benefit program, medications are organized to help members quickly see how costs and coverage will differ from one drug to another. Each group is called a tier, and the tier designates how much a member pays when a prescription is filled.

The tier system is simple. Most common, everyday medicines that are available as generics fall into the lowest tier. The next tier, used for many branded medications, offers a moderate cost share. The highest tier contains specialty drugs that may need extra review before the pharmacy can dispense them. Because these items can be more complex, the plan places them in the top tier.

- Tier 1 generic drugs have a co-payment of 10
- Tier 3 drugs require prior authorisation

Members can review the full formulary online or ask a member service representative. By understanding the tiers, you can plan your pharmacy use and avoid unexpected charges.

## Quantity limits

When you fill a prescription through ClaimSense, the pharmacy benefit program sets clear limits on the quantity you can receive in a single dispensing. These limits help ensure that medication is used responsibly while still providing you with enough supply to meet your health needs. The goal is to balance coverage with cost efficiency, preventing overstocking and waste, and keeping the benefit sustainable for all members.

The standard rule for most medications is that prescriptions are limited to a 90‑day supply. This rule applies to both generic and brand‑name drugs, as well as to most therapeutic classes. If you require a larger quantity for a specific condition, you should discuss options with your prescriber or pharmacy, who can provide guidance on alternate dosing schedules or special access programs.

- Tier A: preferred generics with lowest out‑of‑pocket  
- Tier B: broader brand‑name coverage, moderate cost  
- Tier C: specialty drugs, higher cost, prior authorization required  
- Prescriptions are limited to a 90‑day supply

## Pharmacy claims

Pharmacy claims are the records that show when a member has filled a prescription at a pharmacy. Each claim is a snapshot of the medication, dosage, and quantity that were dispensed.

When a pharmacy submits a claim, the system checks that the details match a prescription that has been approved for the member. If they line up, the claim moves to the next step.

• Tier A: Preferred generic drugs  
• Tier B: Preferred brand drugs  
• Tier C: Non‑preferred drugs  

Every pharmacy claim must match a prescription. If the claim does not align, it is flagged for review and may be denied until the discrepancy is resolved.

Members can review their claim history on the portal and compare it to their prescription records. If any issue arises, a member can contact support to clarify.
