## Assessment

Claims assessment begins when a claim is logged into the system. Every claim is reviewed by a primary assessor who verifies coverage, documentation and eligibility. The assessor assigns a risk rating and determines whether additional evidence is required. When the claim triggers a high‑risk flag, a second review is mandatory. - Claims above 10,000 need a second assessor. Additional assessment steps include:

- Confirm the completeness of the medical report.
- Verify that all supporting invoices are attached.
- Estimate the expected payout range.
- Decide if arbitration or dispute resolution is necessary.

If the primary assessor identifies any discrepancies, they flag the claim for escalation. The second assessor applies an independent audit to ensure consistency. Both assessors collaborate on a final decision and document their findings in the claim file. This collaborative approach guarantees accuracy, fairness and protects the insurer from potential misuse. The final signed assessment is then forwarded to the payment team for settlement. All documents are retained for audit purposes.

## Payment

The payment process begins when a claim passes verification and receives final approval. Once the approval status is recorded, the payment team prepares the remittance. All remittance information must match the claimant’s records exactly. Any discrepancy triggers a review before funds are released.  

The following checklist ensures consistent handling and adherence to policy.  

- Verify approval status and record timestamp  
- Confirm claimant details match the file  
- Prepare payment instructions and attach supporting documentation  
- Submit payment to the finance gateway  
- Confirm receipt confirmation from the claimant

- A denial letter must quote the policy clause that applies

- Offer a brief summary of the decision process
