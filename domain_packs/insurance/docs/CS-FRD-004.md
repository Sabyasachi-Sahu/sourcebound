## Red flags

When reviewing claims, always keep an eye out for subtle patterns that diverge from the norm. Small discrepancies may hint at larger inconsistencies.  

Below are key indicators that should trigger a deeper look. Pay close attention to the details; they often reveal the underlying truth.  

- Unusual medical codes that appear frequently in a short period.  
- Claimants reporting identical or nearly identical symptoms across unrelated visits.  
- Documentation that is incomplete or missing critical signatures.  
- Claimants who are reluctant to provide additional details or who change story mid‑process.  
- Refer the case to the Special Investigations Unit if three or more claimants share one bank account.  
- Payment requests that include unusual routing numbers or foreign currency conversions.  
- Discrepancies between the patient's stated residence and the service location.
